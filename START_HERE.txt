READY FOR GITHUB + VERCEL

1. Open README.md for setup instructions.
2. public/ is the student app. Deploy that output folder.
3. teacher/ contains the Moss answer guide and three-pass review log.

WHAT STUDENTS GET
Beginner: five guided missions, roughly 30 minutes.
Intermediate: five deeper cases, roughly 35–45 minutes.
Advanced: five connected investigations, roughly 45–60 minutes.

Built-in decoders, two kinds of image steganography, network evidence,
progressive hints, local saving, evidence notes and downloadable handoff.

Students join the shared leaderboard with initials; no school account.
Read teacher/LEADERBOARD_SETUP.md to connect the database.
Flag progress syncs online. Evidence notes are NOT automatically sent to Moss. Students download the handoff and submit it as directed.

The app has not been deployed. Full browser/device testing remains.
