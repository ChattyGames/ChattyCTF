YOUR DESTINATION

What is the final ChattyGPT project?

You will publish, demonstrate, or share one original creation connected to an AI topic, opportunity, or question you care about. Your project should have an audience and a purpose—not just show off a tool.

BUILD

A thing people can use

Working app, game, website, assistant, business prototype, trained classifier, or evaluation harness.

CREATE

A thing people can experience

Original music, short film, animation, comic, campaign, interactive story, or multimedia art.

INVESTIGATE

A thing people can learn from

Documentary, news feature, podcast, fact-check, reported article, interview, or facilitated debate.

Every final project will explain: What issue motivated you, what evidence informed you, what you made and why, how you tested or checked it, and what you learned or would improve. You can combine pathways. Building a model and making a documentary are both legitimate.
YOUR MISSION WHILE MOSS IS OUT

Today is idea day—not final-project day.

Keep your original research seed and the categories you already chose. Your earlier research is a starting point, not a restriction.

01 / RECONNECTOpen your ChattyGPT research database. Add or verify a source connected to an AI interest you care about.
02 / EXPLOREVisit at least 2 different pathways below and look at 3 real projects, creators, or debates. Try backup links if blocked.
03 / PITCH TWOFill out Idea A and Idea B in your assigned Creator Studio slide deck. The ideas must use different formats. Idea C is optional.
04 / TEST & SHARETry something small for 10 minutes, get one classmate's reaction, record it, and hit Turn in in Google Classroom.

Source check: If an AI helped you find a source, open the link and verify it exists. Never upload private student data. A blocked or paid site is not required—use the backup or another example.
